Miku Expo 2026 just ended in Los Angeles, and I went on Sunday with my friend mjv2011. If you don't know what Miku Expo is, or what Vocaloids are, basically, Vocaloids are digitized voice instruments that producers can modify to add in their songs. These Vocaloid have a neat character and name attached to them, and there's community surrounding it! The most notable and popular Vocaloids include Hatsune Miku, Kagamine Len, Kagamine Rin, Meiko, and Kaito. Every year, the company that owns Vocaloid, Crypton Future Media, does a concert surrounding some of the top songs that use Vocaloid of that year! This year, Miku Expo 2026, takes place in cities all over America, and mjv2011 and I were able to snag some tickets for the Sunday show.

I was really excited to see Miku Expo this year, partially because I love Vocaloid music, and partially because I've never been to any concert before.
