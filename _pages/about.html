About Me

Heyo, I'm Jake! I usually go by Sirkorgo online, and he's my OC who's a shapeshifting corgi that I like to use as my online persona. I'm obsessed with technology from the 2000's and early 2010's (Apple’s Aqua, the PSP, Nintendo 3DS, that kinda stuff). I love graphic design, web design, some architecture, and a touch of game design (That's a LOT of design).

A little history lesson about me: I’ve always had an interest in tinkering with tech and designing stuff. That tinkering evolved into exploring web design and web infrastructure! One example is well… this site! I’ve been building on it and learning about HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while also exploring some design skills. I also just find it really fun to just open up Photoshop and mock up silly little designs!

In terms of my coding experience, I know how to use CSS and HTML, and a bit of JavaScript. I'm hoping to learn more about JavaScript in the future, and learn some Python, too.

About Sirkorgo

Enough about me, Who and What is sirkorgo? Like I said, Sirkorgo’s a corgi that ate mutated PlayDoh as a child, giving him cool powers to shapeshift! I came up with him back in 2022, and he’s just been my little doodle I draw. Along the way, I came up with new characters! Like Widget, Sirkorgo’s best friend, Mono, Sirkorgo’s sibling, Rabbo, Pip, and Jess! All of that combined into my comic, Cloudspace! (which you should totally read)

About Cloudspace

If you’ve read this far you’ve probably seen Cloudspace mentioned once or twice. Well, what is it? It’s the enclosing world that Sirkorgo and everyone else he knows live in! It’s a world atop clouds powered by a physics-defying energy core that also causes a lot of issues called Anomalies. In other words, paranormal ‘glitches’ in Cloudspace that cause havoc. That’s where Sirkorgo and his team come in. They clean up these anomalies and keep Cloudspace safe.

If that sounded interesting, read it here on sirkorgo.com on the Comics page.
