my music taste had a SUDDEN shift outta nowhere, and i noticed started to enjoy more rock-like j-pop songs. i was getting sick of my old playlist, the sirkorgo & friends remix, so i decided to make a new playlist with a few songs i liked, and with the power of the ALGORITHM i was able to find like a good 34 songs that i liked alongside the other few songs i started out yet.\
\
this artwork is about mono! hes the only one that hasnt gotten his own playlist, so i figured i might as well, right? its not like sirkorgo got 6 different mixtapes dedicated to himself or anything...

my inspo for this was like early 2000s japanese marketing posters, GOD those look so cool. but yeah, i used some helvetica and some pixelated fonts, i think it looks really good, and it fits the sunny vibe of summer\
\
if you're wondering whats ON the playlist, theres artists like yoasobi, yukopi, supercell and deco*27.\
and then i have a few outliers because i just like the tracks, for example:\
toby fox, a few songs from deltarune chapter 5 because holy crap that has a REALLY good soundtrack\
glaDOS, want you gone, it just sounds good, okay?
