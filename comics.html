Meet the Characters!

Sirkorgo

Meet Sirkorgo! He's energetic, a leader, and most importantly, can shapeshift! Sirkorgo loves researching and building little gadgets. If Sirkorgo were to be compared to someone from the Sonic universe, he'd be a lot like Tails!

Widget

Check out Widget! Widget is an experimental Artificial Quantum Lifeform (AQL) in the shape of a UFO. He's a quick thinker, pays attention to small details, and can fly really fast with his built-in motor. Widget and Sirkorgo are best buds and basically inseparable.

Mono

Say hi to Mono! Mono is Sirkorgo's little brother and a remarkable young boy. Mono is a lot smarter than the average middle schooler, so he gets to skip class and tag along with Sirkorgo and Widget occasionally. He may be a bit shy but is still very playful! Despite not having arms or legs, Mono gets around by bouncing, rolling, and when needed, by DIGGING in the ground!
