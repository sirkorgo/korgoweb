At sirkorgo.com, accessible at sirkorgo.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by sirkorgo.com and how I use it.
By using my service, you hereby consent to my Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

1. No Data Collection Policy
   I do not collect, store, or process any personal information, usage data, or user content on my servers. When you use my application to interact with your own Nextcloud or Immich instances, all data remains on your infrastructure.
2. Authentication and Third-Party Apps
   my service acts solely as an intermediary interface to facilitate authentication with your self-hosted apps.

3. Cookies and Tracking
   I do not use cookies or any tracking technologies to monitor user behavior.

5. Children's Information
   I do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
